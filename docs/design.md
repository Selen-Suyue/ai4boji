# AI4薄肌

The title is identical in Chinese and English. First-launch locale: `zh-CN`.

## Homepage palette

Source: [Selen Su / Homepage](https://selen-suyue.github.io/), inspected on 2026-10-16. The three muted accent families follow the homepage's Understanding, Generation and Action filters:

| Token | Color | AI4薄肌 usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--blue` | `#6F8EAE` | Recorded training, secondary chart |
| `--purple` | `#7F7788` | Recovery, calm surfaces and metadata |
| `--rose` | `#A65F68` | Primary actions, active navigation, primary chart |

Use neutral white/near-white surfaces and `#494E52` body text. Softer backgrounds are tints of these families. The homepage's `#CA6F6F` rose shade appears only in small focus/warning accents, within the same rose family. Avoid green, fluorescent colors, large saturated surfaces and unnecessarily heavy shadows.

All chart statuses include text labels. Never use color as the only way to communicate completion, recovery or missing records.
